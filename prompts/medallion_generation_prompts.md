# Промпты для пяти версий механического медальона-карты

Каждый блок ниже — самостоятельный промпт: его можно целиком копировать в генератор изображений. Во всех вариантах главный и самый крупный вид показывает **активированный медальон с детализированной картой**, а не закрытый предмет.

## Вариант 1 — классическая картографическая карта

```text
Create a professional cinematic prop concept sheet in a 16:9 landscape composition for an original mechanical map medallion from a family adventure film. The main and largest image on the sheet must be a perfectly front-facing macro view of the FULLY ACTIVATED medallion, unobstructed and filling most of the frame.

The medallion is a round practical artifact, 55 mm in diameter and 9 mm thick, made of aged warm brass with matte turquoise enamel. It has exactly three broad concentric rotating rings. In the activated state all three rings mechanically align and lock into one flat circular surface. Together they form a highly detailed, crisp and readable engraved plan of a fictional old European city.

The map must contain a dense, believable street network: winding main streets, many narrow alleys of different widths, small lanes, irregular building footprints, compact city blocks, inner courtyards, passages and dead ends. Clearly show a central square with a circular fountain, a stone staircase, a covered arch, a small bridge over a narrow canal, an old tower and an enclosing city wall with gates. Use classical cartographic precision and clear hierarchy between main streets and minor alleys. A single continuous turquoise enamel route begins at the fountain, crosses the boundaries of all three rings without interruption, and ends at a concealed destination beside the tower. Only this route has a very subtle warm amber edge glow.

Every street, building block and landmark is physically engraved into the brass and filled with enamel. The map is built into the surface of the medallion; it is not a projection. Keep the circular seams of all three rings visible while aligning the engraved streets precisely across every seam.

Add five smaller supporting views of the exact same object around the hero view: an extreme macro crop of the activated map, the closed front, the mechanical back, the 9 mm side profile, and the medallion beside a neutral child-sized hand for scale. Neutral dark-gray studio background, soft diffused cinematic product lighting, photorealistic brass, restrained patina, crisp focus, consistent geometry and materials in every view.

Do not create a simplified map, abstract maze, sparse decorative lines, generic compass face, text, letters, numbers, labels, map legend, logos, watermark, real city plan, recognizable real monument, coat of arms, religious or occult symbols, hologram, floating map, projected interface, digital screen, sci-fi HUD, bright energy beams, gemstones, chain, weapon parts, blades, sharp edges, extra rings, fewer than three rings, inconsistent views or an object covering the map.
```
